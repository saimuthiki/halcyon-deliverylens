# Google Flow clips for the DeliveryLens demo film

**Settings for every clip:**
- Model **Veo 3.1 Quality**, or **Veo 3.1 Fast** for the wide shots if credits run low.
- Aspect ratio **16:9 (landscape)**. The film is 1920 x 1080. A 9:16 (portrait) clip would have to be cropped to a thin strip or shown with big black bars.
- Duration **10 seconds**, highest resolution offered.
- The sound is muted in the film, so ignore it.

**Saving:** download each clip and save it as `flow-01.mp4` … `flow-08.mp4` in
`C:\Users\smuthiki\Downloads\halcyon-deliverylens\demo-video\work\flow\`.
If a clip shows readable text, logos, warped hands or strange faces, regenerate that one.

**Shared look (already written into every prompt):** cinematic, anamorphic 35 mm, shallow depth of field,
soft volumetric haze, cool blue-grey light with warm orange accents, slow and steady camera, gentle film grain.
No on-screen text, no brand logos, no readable signs, no close-up faces.

## flow-01: Hook, "Imagine you run a busy shop…" (narration lines 1-4)

A small neighbourhood hardware shop at dawn, seen from inside the back storeroom looking out through an open roller
door onto an empty loading bay. Half-empty shelves in the foreground, a few cardboard boxes, a hand trolley standing
idle. Outside, low ground fog drifts slowly across wet concrete; the sky is pale blue-grey turning slightly warm on the
horizon. A plain round wall clock (no numbers readable, hands pointing near twelve) hangs beside the door.

Camera: a very slow dolly-in from inside the storeroom toward the empty bay over the full 10 seconds, ending on the
empty parking space where a truck should be.

Action timeline:
- 0-4 s: stillness, dust floating in a shaft of light.
- 4-8 s: the fog thickens a little and a distant streetlight flickers off.
- 8-10 s: still empty, nobody arrives.

Mood: quiet, waiting, a little lonely. Cinematic, anamorphic 35 mm, shallow depth of field, soft volumetric haze,
cool blue-grey light with warm orange accents, gentle film grain. No text, no logos, no readable signs, no people.

## flow-02: The scale of the problem, "Halcyon delivers across the United States…" (lines 6-7)

Night-time aerial view high above a large American logistics hub: a huge distribution warehouse with dozens of
loading docks, rows of parked white semi-trailers, and highways leaving it in several directions. Sodium street
lights glow warm orange; the warehouse roof and yards are cool blue. Thin mist lies over the ground. Headlights of
trucks move slowly along the highways like streams of light, some steady, a few stopping and bunching up as if
delayed.

Camera: a slow, smooth drone push forward and slight tilt down over 10 seconds, revealing more highways toward the
horizon.

Mood: vast, busy, slightly tense. Cinematic, anamorphic, volumetric haze, cool blue night with warm orange lights,
film grain. No text, no logos, no readable signs.

## flow-03: Inside the warehouse, "Five causes…" / stock (lines 16-18)

Wide shot inside a modern distribution warehouse just before sunrise. Very tall steel pallet racks stretch into the
distance, a few aisles clearly half-empty with bare shelves, others full of brown cartons. Conveyor belts in the
middle start to move slowly. Light beams from high windows cut through soft haze; a single forklift with its warning
light glowing amber moves slowly across the far end of the aisle.

Camera: a slow crane-up from floor level to high above the racks over 10 seconds, revealing the size of the building.

Mood: calm before the rush, slightly uncertain. Cinematic, anamorphic 35 mm, shallow depth of field, volumetric light

## flow-04: The orders arrive, "Orders arrive by email, in fourteen different layouts…" (line 22)

## flow-06: The long way, "the truck crosses the country and arrives late" (line 19)

Aerial drone shot of one single white delivery truck driving along a very long, straight, empty highway through a
vast dry landscape with distant mountains. Late golden hour, long shadows stretching across the road, light haze over
the plains. The truck looks small and alone; the road seems endless.

Camera: high drone follow shot from behind and slightly above, slowly rising and pulling back over 10 seconds to show
how far the road goes.

Mood: distance, delay, time slipping away. Cinematic, anamorphic, warm orange sunset light with cool blue shadows,
light haze, film grain. No text, no logos, no readable signs.

## flow-07: The control room, "At eight every morning, the risk board…" (lines 30-31)

A modern logistics control room at dawn. Two people seen from behind (no faces visible) stand in front of a large
curved video wall that shows an abstract glowing map of the United States: many thin teal route lines, a few
highlighted in orange, small pulsing dots at cities. Desks with soft monitor glow in the foreground. Fine haze in the
room makes the screen light visible as soft beams.

Camera: slow push-in between the two people toward the video wall over 10 seconds, the orange routes slowly turning
teal one by one.

Mood: in control, problems spotted early. Cinematic, anamorphic 35 mm, shallow depth of field, cool blue-grey room
with teal and orange screen glow, film grain. No readable text or numbers on the screens, no logos, no faces.

## flow-08: The happy ending, "The truck arrives. And if it is going to be late, you hear first." (lines 40-42)

The same small hardware shop's loading bay from flow-01, now in warm morning sunlight. The fog lifts and clears. A

trolley.

Camera: slow pull-back from the bay over 10 seconds, the scene brightening from cool blue to warm golden light.

Mood: relief, trust, on time. Cinematic, anamorphic 35 mm, shallow depth of field, soft volumetric haze clearing,
warm golden light with cool blue shadows, film grain. No text, no logos, no readable signs, no close-up faces.
